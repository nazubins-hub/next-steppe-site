A sample project, designed for a friend's property to put the full process through a real site.

**The brief.** Natives only. That ruled out one species the site would otherwise have taken, and left 19 that clear the bed's light, elevation and fire placement.

**The site.** A 264 ft² bed (measured on site; it matched the aerial trace to within 4%) on a gentle north-northeast slope, 32 ft from the house. It has a padmount transformer at the road edge, so a utility locate comes before any digging.

**The planting.** 194 plants in drifts, placed on the plan, with a one-inch exchange of soil-compost mix and gravel mulch. The palette leans on plants that hold their form through a dry summer at 9,000 ft: harebell, Rocky Mountain geranium, the columbines, Idaho fescue and pussytoes.
